Privacy by architecture

What happens on your phone, stays on your phone.

01

No account

There is nothing to sign up for. Open the box, and it works.

02

No tracking

No analytics SDKs, no ad networks, no telemetry. Nothing phones home.

03

Encrypted at rest

Your library lives in an SQLCipher-encrypted database — bookmarks, notes and streaks included.

04

Offline inference

The AI model runs through llama.cpp on your device's silicon. Your questions are never uploaded.

Offline out of the box. Optional downloads only: the AI scholar model (once) and Quran/adhan audio — even the recitation model ships inside the APK.

Responsible AI

An AI scholar with receipts — and limits.

01

Verified citations

Every reference is validated against the on-device library before it's shown — invalid citations never render.

02

Honest fallback

When no reliable source is found, the scholar says so plainly instead of generating a confident religious answer.

03

Knows its place

Complex or personal rulings carry a standing caution: AI answers can be mistaken — consult qualified scholars.

04

Open about itself

A built-in “About AI & Sources” page names the models, the sources and what stays on your device. Report any answer in one tap.

Recitation feedback follows the same rule: uncertain words are shown as uncertain — AI feedback never replaces a qualified Qur'an teacher.
